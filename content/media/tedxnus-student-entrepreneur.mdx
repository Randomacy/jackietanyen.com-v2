A TEDx talk given at TEDxNUS — Jackie's first — titled "What Does It Take to Become a Student Entrepreneur?"

Drawing directly on founding fundMyLife while a PhD candidate, the talk digs into whether it's actually possible to run a startup and stay a serious researcher at the same time — the mindset it takes, and the specific ways it can go wrong. fundMyLife itself started as a hackathon prototype that got picked up by the media, which is what pushed him to found the company a few months later.
