In May 2025, Jackie gave a talk at **The One About AI Agents and Imposters**, a community event at Lorong AI (WeWork, 22 Cross St) in Singapore. It was pitched at a 200–300 technical level: enough to explain how the technology works without losing the room.

The talk had four parts:

1. **What is a deepfake?** — synthetic media generated with machine learning, across audio, video, images, and text. Deepfakes aren't new, but it has become far easier to make one.
2. **Live demo** — he cloned a voice in the room in real time, twice.
3. **So what?** — voice fraud, social engineering, and impersonation attacks.
4. **What's holding back mass fraud for now** — real-time speech-to-text and good text-to-speech both exist, but real-time *speech-to-speech* with matching cadence and latency is still brittle. That gap is a temporary reprieve, and it's closing.

It ended on what people and organisations can do about it — verification challenges, call-back protocols, and out-of-band confirmation — rather than just on how scary it is.
