At the ONBOARD Conference during Taipei Blockchain Week 2024, Jackie moderated a panel titled "Networked Intelligence: The Rise of Decentralized AI," billed as thirdlyr's co-founder and CEO.

The panel brought together Dr. Danny Lim (co-founder, Pundi AI), Ming Wen (founder, Twin3.ai), and Jiahao Sun (CEO, FLock.io) — three founders building at the intersection of decentralized infrastructure and AI agents. The session ran on Day 2 of the conference, December 13, 2024.
