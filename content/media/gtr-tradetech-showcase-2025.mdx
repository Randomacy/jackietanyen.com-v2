Global Trade Review covered the GTR Ventures Tradetech Showcase, a pitch platform that gives trade-tech startups a stage in front of the wider trade finance community. BorderDollar was one of the companies presenting.

As BorderDollar's co-founder and CEO, Jackie outlined the company's vision: unlocking working capital for SMEs involved in trade by working with supply chain stakeholders and analysing historical trade data. The stated goal is narrowing the roughly $2.5tn global trade finance gap — the shortfall between the financing SMEs need to trade across borders and what's actually available to them.

Havona Technology won that cycle of the showcase; the piece rounds up several of the participating tradetech companies, BorderDollar included.
