An episode of *Rising Up*, Asia Startup Network's podcast celebrating the different paths founders, operators, and investors take through Asia's startup ecosystem.

Recorded while Jackie was co-founder and CEO of [BorderDollar](/apps/borderdollar), the 32-minute conversation covers starting a first startup as a student — [fundMyLife](/apps/fundmylife-startup), started during the PhD — how to validate a startup idea before committing to it, the pivots BorderDollar went through on the way to its final form, and the role of mentorship.
