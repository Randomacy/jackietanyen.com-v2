Jackie's entry on Forbes' own 30 Under 30 Asia 2018 list, Finance & Venture Capital category — the announcement that anchors most of the press coverage from that period.

The listing credits fundMyLife, the financial-advisor matching platform he co-founded with Wesley Goi, and the personal motivation behind it: his father's diagnosis with lung cancer, and the unsuitable insurance policy that came with it, which pushed Jackie toward building a way for people to get multiple, comparable sources of financial planning advice instead of relying on one agent.
