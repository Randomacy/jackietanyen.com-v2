A TEDx talk given at TEDxNewton, titled "If You Understand Memes, You Understand Web3" — delivered while Jackie was Head of Tribe Academy.

The talk lays out three reasons memes matter to how Web3 communities actually form and spread ideas — treating meme culture not as a distraction from the technology, but as one of the clearest signals of how a crypto-native community thinks and organises itself.
