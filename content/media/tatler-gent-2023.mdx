Tatler Asia named Jackie to its Gen.T List 2023 — one of 40 honourees from Singapore that year, out of a wider Asia-spanning list of young leaders.

The profile covers his work as co-founder and CEO of BorderDollar, tackling the trade finance gap by helping maritime businesses access instant cashflow through alternative financing. It also notes his role designing web2 and web3 educational products for AngelHack — the global developer ecosystem acquired by blockchain accelerator Tribe — including upskilling programmes run with institutes of higher learning and developer engagement work like bootcamps, incubators, and hackathons.
