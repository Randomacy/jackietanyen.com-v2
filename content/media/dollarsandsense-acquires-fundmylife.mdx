Coverage of DollarsAndSense's acquisition of fundMyLife, the financial-advisor matching platform Jackie co-founded with Wesley Goi in 2016. Terms weren't disclosed.

fundMyLife let users ask financial planning questions and get matched with up to three advisors, who they could pick between based on reviews. DollarsAndSense — a personal finance media platform — acquired the company to pair its own content and audience reach with fundMyLife's matching engine. Jackie and Wesley stayed on through the transition to keep continuity for existing users. The deal was widely covered — CrowdfundInsider, Yahoo News Singapore, Entrepreneur, Insurance Business Asia, Fintech Singapore, and e27 all ran versions of the story; this is the Vulcan Post write-up.
