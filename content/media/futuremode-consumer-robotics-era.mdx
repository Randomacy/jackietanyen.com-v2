Day 2 of FUTUREMODE 台灣未來祭 (formerly Taipei Blockchain Week) — Saturday, September 5, 2026 — on the FUTUREMODE Stage at Taipei Expo Park. 35-minute panel, 3:20–3:55pm, tagged Consumer Robotics / AI Hardware / Future Living.

**The premise:** robotics is shifting from industrial applications toward products designed for homes, healthcare, and everyday life. The panel dug into what it will actually take for robots to hit mass adoption.

## the panel

**Jun Liu** (moderator) — Cofounder, [Katena AI](https://www.katena.ai/), a startup building a universal synchronization layer for embodied robotics: full-body capture and multimodal signal fusion that translates human intent into physical robot action, deployed hardware-agnostically across use cases from disaster response to manufacturing and retail.

**Jackie Tan** — Founder, Mind Interface Company.

**San Wong** — Founder/CEO, [R2C2 Limited](https://hk.linkedin.com/company/r2c2-limited), a Hong Kong robotics company behind ARC, a platform that coordinates robots from different brands for collaborative inspection and patrol work. San was the first and only Boston Dynamics Spot developer in Hong Kong, and R2C2 won a CES Innovation Award in the robotics category in 2025.

**KT Lin** (Kai-Tse Lin) — Cofounder/COO, BAMS Tech (Bellwether Advanced Mobility Solutions), part of Bellwether Industries, developing urban aircraft and advanced air mobility designs.
